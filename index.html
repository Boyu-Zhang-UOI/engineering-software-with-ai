Engineering Software with AISpecify. Delegate. Verify.

An upper-division and graduate University of Idaho course on engineering software with AI coding agents, for CS seniors after Software Engineering and for graduate students. CS [4xxx]/[5xxx] · 3 credits · a 16-week semester · a Seminar and a Lab each week · $0 student cost.

Open up the agent, then engineer with it

In week 1 you run, read and extend a small coding agent and read its traces. From then on you use real agents on a real open-source codebase, and you know what they are doing.

Week 1

Verification you can measure

Property-based tests, mutation scores, calibrated evals and CI gates, so that "the tests pass" means something.

Measuring test adequacy

A real codebase, a real team

Your team works in a class fork of an active open-source project, reviews other teams' pull requests, and may contribute upstream where the maintainers welcome disclosed AI help.

The team project

Security before autonomy

Prompt injection, the lethal trifecta and sandboxing come in week 4, before your team gives agents broad access.

Securing agents

Graded on evidence

Commit history, AI logs, reproducible checks and honest limitations count; feature count and token spend don't. Short AI-free quizzes and two oral exams check that you understand what you shipped.

How grading works

Free tools, by design

Free tiers, the GitHub Student Developer Pack, open-source tools and a lab machine with local models, with fallbacks for every layer.

The tool handout
